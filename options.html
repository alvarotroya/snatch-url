Host groups

Hosts that stand in for each other, such as a site and its demo or staging copy. Write one group per line, hosts separated by commas, each host the way the address bar shows it (example.com, localhost:3000), optionally with a scheme (http://localhost:3000) that switching to it also sets. When the tab is on a host from a group, the popup's host cell offers the group's other hosts; picking one is staged like any other edit, and nothing navigates until you press Apply.
